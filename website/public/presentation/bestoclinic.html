Privacy & Security

Your Clinic's Data Stays Yours. Period.

Every clinic on BestoClinic gets its own completely separate, isolated database. No shared tables, no data mixing — your patient records are yours and yours alone.

One Clinic, One Database

Each clinic is automatically provisioned its own dedicated database the moment they sign up. Completely independent.

Encrypted & Role-Protected

All clinical data is encrypted. Every action is permission-checked. Each staff member only sees what they're authorized to.

Platform Separation

Administrative data (plans, subscriptions) lives on a separate platform layer — completely segregated from clinical records.
